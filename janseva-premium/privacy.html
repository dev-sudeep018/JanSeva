JANSEVA INDEPENDENT PROTOTYPE

Privacy notice

JanSeva is an independent demonstration and is not connected to a government department. Do not submit confidential, financial, medical, identity-document, or other highly sensitive information.

Information stored

The service stores the grievance title, category, locality, description, optional name and phone number, reference ID, priority, timestamps, status, and status history in Cloudflare D1.

Public tracking

Anyone who knows a grievance reference ID can view its title, category, locality, status, submission date, and status history. Names, phone numbers, and full descriptions are not returned by the public tracking API.

Officer access

Authenticated officers can review complete records, change status, export cases, and permanently remove records. Officer sessions expire automatically.

Security and retention

Submission limits, server-side validation, encrypted deployment secrets, same-origin checks, and protected officer sessions reduce misuse. Records remain stored until an authorised officer removes them or the service is retired.

Your choices

Name and phone number are optional. Keep the reference ID if you want to track a submission. Because this is a prototype, use sample or non-sensitive information only.
